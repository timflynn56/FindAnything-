VEHICLE BUYING GUIDE

How to Find a Good Used Truck

Used trucks can look great in photos while hiding expensive mechanical problems.

  • Compare similar model years, mileage and trim levels.
  • Inspect frame corrosion and accident history.
  • Review service records and tire wear.
  • Budget for repairs before making an offer.
  • Arrange an independent inspection when possible.
ELECTRONICS GUIDE

Buying a Used iPhone

A low asking price is not enough to prove a phone is a bargain.

  • Check battery health and physical condition.
  • Confirm Activation Lock is removed.
  • Verify the device is not carrier-blocked.
  • Compare storage capacity and model generation.
  • Test cameras, speakers and charging.
TOOLS & EQUIPMENT

Used Tools Worth Considering

Quality used tools can offer substantial savings when condition is verified.

  • Inspect motors, batteries and moving parts.
  • Check replacement-part availability.
  • Compare used prices against new retail prices.
  • Test equipment before purchasing.
  • Consider warranty and return-policy differences.
FARM EQUIPMENT

Shopping for a Used Tractor

Buying the right tractor means looking beyond horsepower.

  • Check operating hours and maintenance records.
  • Inspect hydraulics, tires and loader wear.
  • Confirm attachments are compatible.
  • Review local dealer and parts support.
  • Estimate transportation and repair costs.
BARGAIN HUNTING

How to Spot an Overpriced Listing

Compare like-for-like products rather than relying on the seller's original purchase price.

  • Match exact models and specifications.
  • Account for age and condition.
  • Include shipping, taxes and fees.
  • Look at sold prices when available.
  • Watch for suspiciously low prices and scams.
NEGOTIATION GUIDE

Make a Better Offer

A thoughtful offer is more effective than simply asking for the lowest price.

  • Research comparable asking and sold prices.
  • Identify specific condition concerns.
  • Offer a fair price supported by evidence.
  • Communicate clearly and respectfully.
  • Be prepared to walk away.
SMART SHOPPING DECISIONS

New vs. Used: Which is the better deal?

Buying New

  • Manufacturer warranty may be included.
  • Known condition and full product life.
  • Usually higher purchase price.
  • Retailer return policies may help.

Buying Used

  • Potentially lower purchase price.
  • Condition varies between sellers.
  • Warranty coverage may be limited.
  • Inspection and research matter more.

FindAnything Approach

  • Compare total cost, not just sticker price.
  • Account for age and condition.
  • Consider risk and repair expenses.
  • Choose the best overall value.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Know before you search.

What is FindAnything?

FindAnything is a shopping discovery app designed to help users search supported marketplaces, save listings, analyze potential deals and compare products.

Does FindAnything sell the products?

No. FindAnything is not the seller of third-party marketplace listings. Purchases and transactions take place with the relevant marketplace or seller.

Does the AI guarantee a good deal?

No. AI analysis is an estimate based on available listing information. Buyers should independently verify product condition, seller legitimacy and pricing.

What is Deal Battle?

Deal Battle compares two saved listings and helps explain which one may offer better value based on the information available.
