SHOP SMARTER

Original Shopping Guides.

Practical advice for comparing prices, avoiding bad purchases and finding better value.

Buying a Used Pickup Truck

Price alone never tells the whole story.

Read shopping guide

Start with the vehicle's year, mileage, trim, engine, drivetrain and title status. Compare trucks with similar specifications rather than comparing every F-150 as though it were identical.

Ask about accident history, maintenance, rust, transmission behavior, four-wheel-drive operation and towing use. Inspect the frame and underbody carefully, especially in regions where road salt is common.

Include taxes, registration, tires and likely repairs when calculating total cost. A cheaper truck needing major repairs can be a worse bargain than a better-maintained example.

Before purchasing, consider a professional inspection and verify that the seller has a valid title.

Buying a Used iPhone

Check condition, activation and battery health.

Read shopping guide

Compare the exact model, storage capacity, carrier status and cosmetic condition. Ask for battery health information and whether the phone has been repaired.

Confirm that Activation Lock has been removed and the device is not subject to an account or management lock. Check whether the IMEI is reported lost or stolen and whether any financing obligations remain.

Test the display, cameras, speakers, charging port, buttons and wireless connectivity. Meet safely and avoid sending deposits to unknown sellers.

A good price is only a good deal if the phone can be activated and used as expected.

New vs. Used: Which Is Better?

Look beyond the sticker price.

Read comparison

New products typically offer current warranties, predictable condition and easier returns. Used products can offer significant savings, but their condition and remaining service life vary.

Compare the actual purchase price after taxes, shipping, fees and expected repairs. For used goods, consider whether replacement parts and service are available.

When the used price is close to the new price, warranty coverage and buyer protection may justify buying new.

Shopping for Used Power Tools

Find value without buying someone else's problem.

Read shopping guide

Identify the exact model and compare it with current retail prices. Check batteries, chargers, accessories and compatibility with tools you already own.

Look for damaged housings, worn chucks, abnormal vibration, overheating and unusual noises. If possible, test the tool under normal operating conditions.

For cordless equipment, battery replacement can erase much of the savings. For high-value tools, verify ownership and avoid suspicious listings.

How to Compare Marketplace Listings

Make fair, like-for-like comparisons.

Read shopping guide

Start by confirming that the listings represent comparable products. Match the brand, model, specifications, age and included accessories.

Then compare condition, location, seller information and total cost. A low asking price may not include delivery, missing components or needed repairs.

Use deal scores as decision support, not guarantees. When listing information is incomplete, confidence should be lower and further verification is important.

How to Negotiate a Better Price

Make informed offers without wasting time.

Read shopping guide

Research comparable listings before making an offer. Use actual differences in condition, mileage, accessories or repair needs to support your proposed price.

Be respectful and specific. For example, explain that your offer accounts for replacement tires or missing equipment rather than simply demanding a discount.

Never let pressure to complete a deal override basic safety checks. Confirm the item and seller before paying.

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is FindAnything?

FindAnything is a marketplace shopping app designed to help people search, save, analyze and compare listings across supported sources.

Does FindAnything sell products directly?

No. FindAnything is a discovery and comparison experience. Purchases generally take place with the original seller or marketplace.

Does FindAnything automatically import every listing?

No. Some marketplaces offer connected search results, while others open externally. Listings from external marketplaces are not necessarily imported automatically.

Are AI deal scores guaranteed?

No. Scores are estimates based on available information. Listing details can be inaccurate or incomplete, and buyers should independently verify important facts.
